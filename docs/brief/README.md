*Enterprise Automation, Coordination & Collaboration — Accountably — Through YAML.*

## How to Read This Brief

The brief is layered. Start at the top; go deeper as interest
warrants.

**Layer 0** is the executive summary — positioning, differentiators,
themes, and market context. Most readers stop here.

**Layer 1** expands each capability area — what it does, what makes
it different, how deep it goes. Technical leaders read here.

**Layer 3** is the trajectory — where the platform is heading,
organised by horizon.
